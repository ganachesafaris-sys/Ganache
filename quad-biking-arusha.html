The experience

An adventurous way to see another side of Arusha

Quad biking is a fun way to explore the landscapes surrounding Arusha at a different pace. After a safety briefing and introduction to the bikes, your guide leads you along countryside trails where the scenery can include farmland, local communities and views of Mount Meru.

The experience works well as a standalone Arusha activity or as an addition before or after a longer Tanzania safari.

What to expect

Your quad biking experience

Guided riding

Ride with local guidance throughout the experience, with support available along the route.

Scenic countryside

Explore trails outside central Arusha and enjoy a different view of the region's landscapes.

Mount Meru views

On clear days, the surrounding countryside offers beautiful views towards Mount Meru.

Flexible planning

Ganache Safaris can help arrange the activity within a wider Arusha stay or Tanzania safari itinerary.

Who it suits

Perfect for travellers looking for a little adventure

Quad biking is a good choice for couples, friends, families and small groups looking for an active experience around Arusha. You do not need to be an expert rider, but you should be comfortable following the guide's safety instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Planning your quad biking experience

Where does the quad biking experience take place?

The activity takes place in the Arusha area, using countryside trails selected for the experience. Ganache Safaris will confirm the meeting point and arrangements when you enquire.

Can beginners participate?

Beginners can usually participate after receiving the required safety briefing and instructions. Final participation requirements are confirmed when booking.

Can transport be arranged?

Ganache Safaris can help coordinate transport depending on your pickup location and the package selected.

Can I combine quad biking with another Arusha activity?

Yes. The experience can be incorporated into a wider Arusha itinerary alongside other activities and safari planning.
