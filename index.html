We plan Tanzania the way we know it

Ganache Safaris is based in Arusha, at the foot of Mount Meru and the gateway to Tanzania's northern parks. We are locally owned and locally run, and we plan trips from the ground up rather than from a catalogue.

That means routes shaped by the season, guides who read the land daily, and time built in to slow down. We travel with respect for the wildlife and the communities we visit — the kind of journey we would want a friend to have.

The Tanzania journey

Climb. Explore. Unwind.

Tanzania rewards travellers who combine its landscapes. A single trip can move from a mountain to the plains to the ocean — designed around you, not squeezed into a fixed package.

01 · CLIMB

The mountains

Kilimanjaro or Mount Meru — a supported walk through forest, moorland and open ridge.

02 · EXPLORE

The wild

Game drives across the Serengeti, Ngorongoro and Tarangire, at the pace the wildlife sets.

03 · UNWIND

The coast

Finish on Zanzibar's beaches — warm water, quiet sand and time to do nothing at all.
