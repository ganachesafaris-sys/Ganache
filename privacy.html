Privacy

PLACEHOLDER — Ganache team: please review this with your own preferences (and, if needed, a local legal adviser) before publishing. It is a plain, honest starting point, not formal legal advice.

What we collect

When you use the planning form or contact us, we collect only what you choose to share — such as your name, email, phone number, country and details about the trip you're planning.

How we use it

We use your details for one purpose: to reply to you and help plan your trip. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone who isn't directly involved in arranging your travel.

How enquiries reach us

Enquiries reach us by email, WhatsApp, or through a secure form service if one is connected. Messages you send on WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Keeping your details

We keep your enquiry for as long as we need it to help with your trip and normal follow-up. If you'd like us to delete your details, just ask.

Contact

Questions about your information? Email or message us on WhatsApp.
